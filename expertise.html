Our Areas
of Expertise

We integrate legal considerations into business strategy, helping you operate with clarity, navigate complexity confidently, and unlock opportunities from day one through exit.

Company Incorporation and Structuring

We help set up and structure businesses — locally and internationally — so you are ready to scale and raise capital.

  • Company incorporation (local and international)
  • Founders' agreements
  • Cap table planning
  • Group structuring and reorganisation
  • Legal frameworks for operational scalability and investor-readiness

Funding and Investment Rounds

We guide you through every stage of fundraising — so each round is clear, fair, and future-proof.

  • SAFEs, convertibles & term sheets
  • Equity rounds (from pre-Seed to Seed and Series A/B/C and beyond)
  • Venture debt & credit facilities
  • Investor negotiations & governance
  • Shareholders' agreements & investor rights

Hiring and Talent

We cover the legal side of hiring, equity plans, and working with contractors — so you build the right team with confidence.

  • Employment law & contracts
  • Contractor, advisor & consultant agreements
  • ESOPs & VSOPs design and implementation
  • Cross-border hiring & compliance
  • Handbooks & onboarding

Commercial Contracts and Day-to-Day Operations

From everyday agreements to commercial partnerships — contracts that support your product, platform, and operations.

  • Day-to-day corporate affairs & governance
  • Services agreements & licensing
  • Terms of use & privacy policies
  • Client, partner & supplier agreements
  • Joint ventures & collaborations
  • GDPR & data protection

IP and Technology

We protect what you build — covering IP ownership, licensing, confidentiality, and open-source risk.

  • IP strategy & ownership structuring
  • Software licensing & IP assignment
  • NDAs & confidentiality
  • Trademark filings
  • Open-source risk analysis

Cross-Border Expansion

We guide your international growth — foreign entities, global contracts, and cross-border compliance.

  • Legal structuring for international growth
  • Foreign entities & subsidiaries
  • Coordination with local counsel
  • Cross-border contracts & risk allocation
  • Tax & employment alignment
  • Market entry strategies

M&A, Exits and Strategic Transactions

We structure, negotiate, and close M&A, exits, and strategic transactions — across borders and time zones.

  • Due diligence (buy- & sell-side)
  • M&A, SPAs & earn-outs
  • Exits, secondaries & post-closing matters
  • Corporate reorganisations & restructuring
  • Transaction insurance (W&I)

Ongoing Legal Support

Consistent, ongoing support as your organisation grows — we stay by your side beyond the deal.

  • Day-to-day legal advisory
  • Corporate housekeeping & governance
  • Regulatory & compliance
  • Contract reviews & updates
  • Support for leadership, finance & HR teams

How We Work?

We are driven by a passion for making things happen. That is why we:

  1. Deliver business-driven legal solutions

    Always thinking two steps ahead. We focus on what matters now, while anticipating what comes next to protect your future and support your momentum.

  2. Structure legal frameworks

    We ensure your legal setup works for your business — enabling smooth operations, informed decisions, and sustainable growth.

  3. Draft with clarity, precision, and purpose

    Always driven by what matters most. Our documents are designed to be understood and used, and focused on the underlying objectives of your team, your partners, your investors — whoever needs to act on them.

  4. Negotiate with a focus

    We stay focused on your priorities and outcomes — creating value, protecting your interests, and keeping deals on track.

  5. Close deals seamlessly

    We coordinate across borders, time zones, and teams to keep transactions moving smoothly from negotiation to closing.

  6. Stay by your side

    Supporting your business in what comes next. Your business evolves, and so does our support. We're not just here for the deal — we are your long-term partner in law and business.
